    Privacy

    OpenSurf has no accounts, telemetry, analytics, ads or remote configuration. It only connects to the pages you open.

    Clear browsing data?

    This removes cookies, cached files, site data (local storage, IndexedDB, service workers), back/forward history, recently closed tabs, the downloads list and site permissions. Open tabs stay open and downloaded files are kept.
